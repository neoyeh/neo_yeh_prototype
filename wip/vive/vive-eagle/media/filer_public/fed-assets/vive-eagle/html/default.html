Where can I try VIVE Eagle?

Where can I buy VIVE Eagle?

Find Taiwan stores
Does VIVE Eagle come in different sizes? Which size suits me the most?
VIVE Eagle is available in two sizes: M and L. We recommend visiting a store to find the best fit for you.
Do you need a smartphone to use VIVE Eagle?
Yes, a smartphone is necessary to use VIVE Eagle. To set up VIVE Eagle, you will need to download VIVE Connect (available on the Google Play Store or App Store) on your phone.
What's included in the VIVE Eagle box?
You’ll find the following items in the box included with your VIVE Eagle purchase:
  • VIVE Eagle AI glasses
  • Charging cable
  • Glasses case
